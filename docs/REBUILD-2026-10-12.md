- Battle Party now displays only the current party in a compact 1–6-row menu. Fainted members remain visible but cannot switch in. The PC’s full-species selector remains separate. B cancels without switching or spending a turn.
- Four existing PP words now store `move ID * 100 + remaining PP`. Evolution preserves those identities and depleted PP, learns only the evolved species’ move at the exact level, and retains inherited moves through healing and subsequent learning. The existing retired word carries the format marker and persistent status. No new save/VM variable slots are allocated.

- Critical probability uses base Speed / 2, with the four high-critical moves multiplied by eight and capped at 255. Critical damage doubles effective level before the calculation and ignores modeled stage/guard reductions. Immune hits cannot announce a critical hit. Both sides have critical rolls.
- Enemy paralysis, poison, sleep and their implemented secondary chances persist per species across switching, evolution and serialized source state. Immunities and existing primary status are respected. Sleep/paralysis can prevent action without consuming PP; poison residual damage clamps and persists HP. Healing cures status. Other original move effects remain simplified or unsupported as described by the existing effect policy.
- Thunder Wave Ground immunity, physical Growl reduction, and scripted Snorlax/legendary species HP repairs remain intact.
- Conditional scene entry restores the defeated Bridge Rocket and Fisherman passages and deactivates captured Snorlax. Generator source now also preserves the safe Celadon hideout doorstep and S.S. Anne pier return instead of undoing native-only repairs during regeneration.
